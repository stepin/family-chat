For one family, from two to twenty people

A family chat that lives in your home

Family Chat: your family's messages on a program that runs in your home. Messages are encrypted on the phone; the server stores only ciphertext.

An administrator (usually one of the parents) runs the server, everybody else joins with an invitation link. No phone number and no app store. The app speaks English and Russian.

What it looks like

The app opens in the browser and installs on the home screen of a phone or a computer.

The screenshots are made automatically from a real server with invented data: the Sokolov family.

What it does

What a family needs every day, and nothing else.

  • Chats and groupsDirect chats, groups and "Saved messages" for notes to yourself. Replies, edits, delete for everyone, reactions, emoji, stickers, forwarding.
  • Photos and voiceFiles up to 200 MB (the administrator sets the limit), voice messages up to 10 minutes, places without loading a map.
  • End-to-end encryptionText, files and group names are encrypted on the device.
  • Search on the deviceSearch through the history runs on the phone; the query goes nowhere.
  • NotificationsAbout new messages (Web Push). On iPhone only for the app on the home screen.
  • An AI assistantClaude, for example, in the family chat. It connects over the MCP protocol, and you decide what it may do.

Works on iPhone, Android and computers as a web app (PWA). One app can hold several families (servers).

What it does not do

  • No calls or video calls. No read receipts and no "typing…".
  • The server keeps messages for at most 90 days (or less). Everything older lives only on devices and in archive files.
  • Lost a phone without the 24 recovery words and without an archive: the older history is gone.
  • The server does not obtain an HTTPS certificate by itself: you need a reverse proxy, a router with HTTPS, or your own certificate files.
  • One device gets notifications from one family only.

In detail, what the server sees and what encryption does not protect: Security.

An AI assistant in the family

You can connect an AI assistant, Claude for example, to the family chat. It reads and writes messages, runs groups, sends files, and helps the administrator with invitations. No separate program is needed: it is built into the server.

  • A helper with its own identity. A separate family member with a name of its own. An administrator creates it; it sees only what people write to it.
  • My identity. The assistant reads your chats and writes in your name. Each person decides that for themselves.
  • Permissions in the app. Reading and writing are on; files, administration and irreversible actions are switched on by a person. The assistant cannot change them itself.

The price of the convenience: to act for an identity, the server keeps its key and can read its chats. Relatives see a note in the profile of such a member. More: AI assistants (MCP).

How it works

Four steps from a box on the shelf to the first "did you get home ok?".

  1. A server at homeOne program, fcd, and one data folder, no database. Any computer that runs around the clock will do: a mini PC, NAS, Raspberry Pi, home server or VPS. There are builds for Linux (x86_64, ARM64, ARMv7), macOS, Windows and a Docker image.
  2. HTTPSThe browser requires a secure connection for encryption, notifications and installing the app. Usually a reverse proxy provides it (Caddy, nginx, a NAS, a router).
  3. The familyThe administrator opens the server address, enters the code from the server log, writes down the 24 recovery words and creates invitations. Each invitation works for one person for 7 days.
  4. ChattingA relative opens the link, installs the app, enters a name and writes. History lives on their device; the server passes on what is new and deletes it after 90 days.
